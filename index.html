GET TO KNOW US

A Church Built on Faith and Love

Jesus Is Lord Church - Compostela is a community of believers committed to worshiping God, growing in faith, and sharing the message of Jesus Christ.

We believe that the church is more than a building. It is a family where people can find encouragement, fellowship, spiritual growth, and opportunities to serve.
